The Problem

EuroPharm LTD, a leading B2B pharmaceutical distributor supplying hospitals and pharmacies across Lebanon, held years of ERP sales and purchase data with no analytical framework. Lebanon's economic collapse, currency depreciation, and post-COVID supply chain disruptions made data-driven decisions urgent. I designed and executed the full pipeline solo, delivering findings to both company management and academic supervisors under a formal NDA.

Analyses Conducted

  • Sales trend and seasonal pattern analysis (2022 to 2024)
  • Product-level profitability and P and L across full catalog
  • Customer segmentation via RFM analysis and K-Means clustering
  • Churn prediction with logistic regression, iterated and refined
  • Sales forecasting: ARIMA, Prophet and Exponential Smoothing
  • Cohort analysis across 12 cohorts, inventory DSI, anomaly detection

Key Findings

  • Amoklavin 1000mg was the most profitable SKU at 50.6 billion LBP in margin. The American University of Beirut Medical Center was the top client, confirming the B2B hospital segment as EuroPharm's strategic core.
  • Built two churn prediction models using the same RFM features. The initial model surfaced almost no at-risk clients. After iterating on training methodology, the refined model successfully identified 129 clients at genuine risk of leaving, demonstrating how model design drives outcomes as much as feature selection.
  • ARIMA captured seasonal patterns with the least lag compared to Prophet and Exponential Smoothing. January consistently showed peak sales driven by post-holiday hospital stockpiling and Lebanese customs batch-release dynamics.
  • Cohort retention dips at month 3 then recovers above baseline by month 10. Timing retention campaigns at the month 3 inflection point was the primary strategic recommendation to management.

Deployed Streamlit Application

Built an interactive web application allowing EuroPharm's non-technical team to filter data by year, client, and product, run live RFM analysis, and generate real-time forecasts using Prophet and Exponential Smoothing without writing a single line of code. This transforms a one-time analysis into a permanent operational tool.

03 · Healthcare Analytics · 2024 · Solo

Healthcare Analytics for Suicide Prevention in Sweden

MSc Healthcare Analytics · AUB Mediterraneo

Tableau DashboardWHO · OECD · IHME Public HealthMental Health

The Paradox

Sweden ranks among the world's highest in quality of life, yet persistent suicide rates tell a more complex story. This project used healthcare analytics and a Tableau dashboard to investigate the relationship between mental illness, depression, social media use, and suicide rates across gender groups, age brackets, and time, producing data-driven prevention strategies for a country that should theoretically have the resources to address them.

Key Findings

  • Males accounted for two-thirds of all suicides, consistent across every age group, pointing to a structural gap in mental health outreach for men.
  • Depressive symptoms among young adults are rising despite declining overall trends in Europe, strongly correlated with heavy social media use.
  • Suicide rates significantly higher among individuals with pre-secondary education and in rural areas, identifying socioeconomic access as the primary prevention lever.
